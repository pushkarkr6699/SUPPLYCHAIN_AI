# Dataset handoff

**Current integration:** all 26 supplied artifacts are imported and registered. d1 primary orders and final scores are active; d2 web-visit forecasts are active; d3 remains separate. Both trained models are validated and available through UI prediction controls. Follow [FINAL_HANDOFF.md](../docs/FINAL_HANDOFF.md) and [QA_REPORT.md](../QA_REPORT.md) for inputs and current executed validation.

Active delivery inputs are `delivery/final/DataCo_Final_Order_Level_Dataset.csv` (65,752 unique orders) and `delivery/final/DataCo_Final_Scored_Orders.csv` (2,123 final scores). The earlier `DataCo_Late_Delivery_Predictions.csv` is reference only. Evaluation files are under `delivery/final/evaluation/`. Active demand is `demand/final/AccessLogs_Final_Advanced_Forecast.csv` (2,280 product/base-day rows, next-day web visits).

Models are under `models/delivery/` and `models/demand/`; notebooks provide training provenance. d3 remains a separate line-item reference under `delivery/legacy/d3/`. Its duplicate demand forecast is preserved as a reference. No partial `.crdownload` file is used. Unscored orders retain missing probabilities. Delivery and demand are not joined without a shared key and compatible grain.

For future inputs, supply original named columns and document target definition, row grain/key, date meaning, data splits and prediction provenance. Daily demand uploads need `DateOnly, Product, Category, Department, Visits`, zero-visit days and at least 15 consecutive days per product. Delivery inference requires the 26 features in `services/inference_service.py`. New analytical snapshots or models require registry/schema review and revalidation. Copying arbitrary files does not silently activate them. Fixed trusted model artifacts may deserialize fitted preprocessors; uploaded pickles are unsupported. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

Profitability CSV/TXT references are under `data/profitability/final/`, with the original model under `models/profitability/`. Final delivery files supplied under `DATACO FINAL` are exact duplicates of the registered d3 files under `data/delivery/legacy/d3/` and `models/delivery/d3/`; registry source aliases record both locations. The legacy folder is provenance placement, not an indication that the independent experiment is hidden from the UI. Sources are immutable and Git-ignored.
